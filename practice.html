NCERT content composer

How to add a stage: Pick a chapter & stage, paste an NCERT paragraph, and wrap the answer words in square brackets — e.g. The [mitochondria] is the powerhouse of the cell.
New: add a few distractor words below (wrong but believable options like "ribosome, lysosome"). They get mixed into the word bank so students must actually know the answer, not just sort the words.

📦 Bulk add many stages at once

Paste multiple stages separated by a line with just ---. Start each block with a header line: 11-1 | 1 | fill | 180 (chapter | stage | type | timer), then the content lines. Great for building the whole syllabus fast.
